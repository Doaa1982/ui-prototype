# About This Document

## Purpose

This document defines the Learning Experience provided by the platform.

While the Educational Provider Model describes how education is created and delivered, the Learning Experience Domain describes how learners discover, participate in, experience, and benefit from education throughout their lifelong learning journey.

The platform aims to create engaging, personalized, and continuously improving learning experiences rather than simply delivering educational content.

# Guiding Principles

## Learning is a Journey

Learning is a continuous journey rather than a collection of isolated lessons.

Every educational experience contributes to long-term personal and professional growth.

---

## Learners are Active Participants

Learners actively construct knowledge through participation, reflection, practice, collaboration, and feedback.

The platform encourages active engagement rather than passive content consumption.

---

## Every Learner is Different

Each learner has unique goals, interests, prior knowledge, learning preferences, and educational needs.

The platform should adapt learning experiences without compromising educational quality.

---

## Learning Should Be Personalized

Learning experiences should evolve based on learner progress, goals, educational evidence, and recommendations.

Personalization should support learning rather than limit exploration.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Learning experiences continuously improve through:

- learner feedback
- Educational Intelligence
- analytics
- educator observations
- assessment outcomes
- educational reviews

---

## Human Relationships Matter

Technology enhances learning but does not replace meaningful interactions between learners and educators.

Trust, encouragement, mentorship, and communication remain essential components of successful education.

# SECTION I — Learning Foundation

Learning is the central purpose of the platform.

Every feature, service, recommendation, interaction, and educational asset ultimately exists to help learners achieve meaningful educational outcomes.

The Learning Foundation establishes the principles that guide every learning experience across the platform.

Rather than viewing learners as consumers of educational content, the platform recognizes learners as active participants in an evolving educational journey.

---

# 1. Learning Experience

## Purpose

The Learning Experience represents the complete educational journey from discovering learning opportunities to achieving educational goals and continuing lifelong learning.

It encompasses every interaction between learners, educational providers, educational assets, communities, and Educational Intelligence.

---

## Definition

A Learning Experience is the collection of educational activities, interactions, services, and support that enable learners to acquire knowledge, develop skills, achieve competencies, and grow personally or professionally.

Learning Experiences may occur independently, collaboratively, synchronously, asynchronously, online, or through blended learning.

---

## Characteristics

A high-quality Learning Experience is:

- learner-centred
- engaging
- purposeful
- inclusive
- accessible
- personalized
- collaborative
- measurable
- continuously improving

# 2. Learning Philosophy

The platform believes that meaningful learning extends beyond completing lessons or passing assessments.

Learning is an ongoing process of curiosity, practice, reflection, feedback, and improvement.

Educational Providers, Educational Intelligence, and the platform collaborate to support learners throughout this journey.

---

## Educational Beliefs

The platform is founded on the following beliefs.

### Learning is Active

Learners construct understanding through participation, experimentation, collaboration, and reflection.

Passive consumption alone rarely produces lasting understanding.

---

### Learning is Personal

Every learner brings unique experiences, motivations, strengths, and aspirations.

The platform should respect these differences while maintaining educational standards.

---

### Learning Requires Practice

Knowledge becomes meaningful through application.

Learning Experiences should encourage learners to practice, experiment, and receive constructive feedback.

---

### Reflection Strengthens Learning

Reflection enables learners to consolidate understanding, identify challenges, celebrate achievements, and plan future learning.

The platform should encourage regular reflection throughout the educational journey.

---

### Lifelong Learning

Learning does not end with completing a Program or earning a certificate.

The platform encourages continuous personal and professional development throughout life.
