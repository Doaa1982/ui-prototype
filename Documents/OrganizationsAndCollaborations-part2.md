# SECTION II — Collaboration

Collaboration enables multiple educators to combine their expertise, share resources, review work, and continuously improve educational quality within an organization.

---

# 6. Collaboration

## Purpose

Collaboration connects educators, managers, and reviewers in the shared creation and delivery of educational content.

---

## Definition

Collaboration is the collective process of planning, authoring, refining, and delivering educational experiences within an Organization.

---

## Characteristics

- Multi-author contribution to Programs, Courses, and Units.
- Shared visibility of educational assets across organizational members.
- Role-based collaboration (e.g. Lead Author, Co-Author, Reviewer, Contributor).
- Preserves individual attribution while contributing to organizational value.

---

# 7. Shared Educational Assets

## Purpose

Organizations build long-term value by creating an internal library of shared educational assets.

---

## Definition

Shared Educational Assets are Programs, Courses, Units, Lessons, Activities, Assessments, and Resources owned by or shared within an Organization.

---

## Characteristics

- Reusable across multiple Courses and Programs within the Organization.
- Access control governed by organizational roles.
- Centralized updating: improvements benefit all referencing courses (with versioning controls).
- Asset templates that maintain organizational teaching standards.

---

# 8. Collaborative Content Creation

## Purpose

Streamlines how teams of tutors and instructional designers build complex curricula together.

---

## Definition

Collaborative Content Creation is the workflow and mechanisms supporting concurrent or sequential authoring of educational content.

---

## Workflow

```text
Planning & Structure
        │
        ▼
 Concurrent Authoring (Tutors & Instructional Designers)
        │
        ▼
   Asset Assembly (Lessons, Activities, Resources)
        │
        ▼
  Internal Review (Peer & Educational Manager)
```

---

# 9. Internal Reviews

## Purpose

Internal Reviews maintain high educational quality, pedagogical alignment, and content consistency before publishing.

---

## Definition

An Internal Review is a structured evaluation of educational assets by peers, educational managers, or Educational Intelligence within the organization.

---

## Characteristics

- Constructive feedback on clarity, alignment, difficulty, and pacing.
- Multi-perspective evaluation (Pedagogical, Factual, Visual, Assessment quality).
- Review status states (Draft, Under Review, Changes Requested, Approved).

---

# 10. Internal Communication

## Purpose

Internal Communication supports ongoing coordination and knowledge sharing among organizational members.

---

## Definition

Internal Communication encompasses contextual discussions, feedback threads, and announcements attached directly to educational assets or organizational workspaces.

---

## Characteristics

- Contextual comment threads on specific Units, Lessons, or Resources.
- Announcements for organizational policy updates or curriculum changes.
- Direct feedback between Educational Managers and Tutors.

---

# SECTION III — Educational Governance

Educational Governance ensures that an Organization maintains consistent academic standards, quality control, and publishing oversight.

---

# 11. Educational Governance

## Purpose

Educational Governance provides the framework for academic quality, accountability, and organizational consistency.

---

## Definition

Educational Governance is the policies, roles, and workflows that regulate content creation, quality standards, and publishing permissions in an Organization.

---

## Principles

- Academic Integrity: Quality comes before quantity.
- Clear Responsibilities: Defined roles for creation, review, and approval.
- Continuous Quality Audit: Ongoing evaluation of live educational assets.

---

# 12. Educational Standards

## Purpose

Educational Standards establish a benchmark for quality, format, tone, and pedagogical methodology across the organization.

---

## Definition

Educational Standards are guidelines and criteria defined by an Organization to ensure educational consistency.

---

## Elements

- Curriculum structure and learning outcome expectations.
- Tone, branding, and presentation guidelines.
- Assessment difficulty calibration and rubric guidelines.
- Mandatory accessibility and language standards.

---

# 13. Publishing Governance

## Purpose

Publishing Governance controls when and how educational content becomes available to students or the public marketplace.

---

## Definition

Publishing Governance is the approval process and policy enforcement required before content is published.

---

## Publishing Workflow

```text
Draft Created by Tutor
          │
          ▼
    Internal Review
          │
          ▼
Approval by Educational Manager / Owner
          │
          ▼
   Published Content
```
