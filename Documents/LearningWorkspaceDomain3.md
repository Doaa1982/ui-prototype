# SECTION III — Personalized Learning

Personalized Learning enables the platform to adapt educational experiences to the unique characteristics, goals, progress, and preferences of each learner.

Rather than providing identical learning experiences for everyone, the platform combines learner-defined information, Educational Knowledge, and Educational Intelligence to recommend meaningful learning opportunities while preserving learner autonomy and educational quality.

Personalization supports learners throughout their educational journey but never replaces learner choice or educator judgement.

# 11. Personalized Recommendations

## Purpose

Personalized Recommendations help learners discover educational opportunities that align with their goals, interests, competencies, and educational progress.

Recommendations support learner decision-making without limiting exploration or learner choice.

---

## Recommendation Sources

Recommendations may consider:

- Learner Profile
- Learning Goals
- Learning Preferences
- Educational Record
- Learning Insights
- Educational Provider recommendations
- Organization requirements
- Marketplace availability

Recommendations should consider multiple sources whenever possible.

---

## Recommendation Categories

### Learning Opportunities

Examples include:

- Programs
- Courses
- Units
- Lessons
- Learning Paths

---

### Educational Providers

Examples include:

- Tutors
- Organizations
- Academies
- Training Centres

Recommendations may consider teaching style, learner feedback, educational quality, and subject expertise.

---

### Learning Resources

Examples include:

- videos
- articles
- books
- activities
- practice exercises
- reference materials

---

### Communities

Examples include:

- study groups
- discussion communities
- learning circles
- peer mentoring

---

### Events

Examples include:

- webinars
- workshops
- live sessions
- office hours
- educational events

---

## Recommendation Principles

Recommendations should:

- remain transparent
- explain why they were suggested
- avoid repetitive recommendations
- support learner goals
- encourage exploration
- evolve continuously

Learners remain free to ignore recommendations.

# 12. Learning Plans

## Purpose

Learning Plans help learners organize their educational journey into achievable and meaningful steps.

A Learning Plan translates long-term Learning Goals into practical educational activities.

Learning Plans may be created by learners, Educational Providers, Organizations, or Educational Intelligence.

---

## Learning Plan Components

A Learning Plan may include:

- Learning Goals
- recommended Programs
- Courses
- Units
- milestones
- target dates
- study schedule
- revision activities
- assessments
- competency milestones

---

## Learning Plan Types

### Personal Learning Plans

Created and managed by learners.

---

### Tutor Learning Plans

Prepared by tutors for individual learners.

---

### Organization Learning Plans

Required learning paths assigned by Organizations.

---

### AI-Assisted Learning Plans

Generated by Educational Intelligence based on learner goals and educational evidence.

Learners remain responsible for approving AI-generated plans.

---

## Plan Lifecycle

```text
Create Plan

↓

Study

↓

Track Progress

↓

Review

↓

Adjust

↓

Complete

↓

Create Next Plan
```

Learning Plans evolve throughout the learner's educational journey.

# 13. Accessibility

## Purpose

Accessibility ensures that every learner can participate in educational experiences regardless of physical, cognitive, sensory, linguistic, or technological needs.

Accessibility is a core educational principle rather than an optional feature.

---

## Accessibility Areas

Examples include:

### Visual Accessibility

- high contrast
- scalable text
- screen reader compatibility

---

### Hearing Accessibility

- captions
- transcripts
- visual indicators

---

### Cognitive Accessibility

- simplified layouts
- structured navigation
- reduced distractions
- additional explanations

---

### Learning Accessibility

Examples include:

- extended assessment time
- alternative learning resources
- multiple content formats
- flexible pacing

---

## Accessibility Principles

Accessibility should:

- be configurable
- remain consistent
- respect learner preferences
- support Educational Providers
- comply with recognized accessibility standards where applicable

---

# 14. Communication Preferences

## Purpose

Communication Preferences define how learners wish to receive educational communication throughout their learning journey.

They improve learner engagement while respecting learner choice.

---

## Communication Categories

Examples include:

- tutor messages
- organization announcements
- assignment reminders
- assessment reminders
- weekly summaries
- achievement notifications
- community updates
- marketing communications

---

## Delivery Channels

Examples include:

- in-platform notifications
- email
- mobile notifications
- SMS (where supported)

---

## Communication Principles

Communication should:

- remain relevant
- avoid unnecessary interruptions
- respect learner preferences
- support educational success
- distinguish required communications from optional communications

Mandatory educational communications may override optional notification preferences where appropriate.
