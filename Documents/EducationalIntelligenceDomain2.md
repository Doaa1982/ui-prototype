# SECTION III — LEARNER INTELLIGENCE

Learner Intelligence supports students throughout their educational journey by providing personalized guidance, interactive practice, and targeted intervention.

---

# 8. Student Support

## Purpose

Provides learners with instant assistance, concept clarifications, and practice opportunities during their study sessions.

---

## Definition

Student Support Intelligence acts as a 24/7 learning companion that helps students overcome learning obstacles without replacing live tutor interaction.

---

## Capabilities

- **Instant Concept Explanation**: Explains complex topics using alternative analogies or simpler language.
- **Socratic Tutoring**: Guides students toward answers using probing questions rather than directly providing answers.
- **Study Preparation**: Generates personalized revision summaries and practice quizzes before assessments.

---

# 9. Personalization

## Purpose

Adapts learning experiences to individual student goals, preferences, background knowledge, and learning pace.

---

## Definition

Personalization Intelligence tailors content recommendations, activity difficulty, and feedback style to suit each learner.

---

## Characteristics

- Recommends supplementary units or remedial activities based on performance.
- Adjusts question difficulty dynamically based on historical mastery.
- Respects student learning preferences (e.g. visual explanations, text summaries, hands-on practice).

---

# 10. Adaptive Learning

## Purpose

Creates dynamic learning pathways that evolve automatically based on real-time student mastery and assessment feedback.

---

## Definition

Adaptive Learning Intelligence adjusts the sequence of Units and Lessons to ensure optimal challenge and retention for each learner.

---

## Adaptive Flow

```text
Concept Presentation
        │
        ▼
   Assessment
        │
  ┌─────┴─────┐
  ▼           ▼
Mastered   Struggling
  │           │
  ▼           ▼
Advance    Remedial Unit / Alternative Explanation
```

# SECTION IV — ORGANIZATIONAL INTELLIGENCE

Organizational Intelligence captures, preserves, and scales institutional knowledge across an Organization.

---

# 11. Organizational Knowledge

## Purpose

Preserves an organization's unique pedagogical style, terminology, brand tone, and institutional expertise.

---

## Definition

Organizational Knowledge Intelligence builds a semantic knowledge base from an organization's published courses, guides, and templates.

---

## Characteristics

- Ensures AI assistance reflects the organization's specific teaching methodology.
- Maintains consistent terminology across different tutors and courses.
- Protects institutional memory when tutors join or leave the organization.

---

# 12. Curriculum Intelligence

## Purpose

Helps academic leaders identify gaps, redundancies, and alignment issues across institution-wide curricula.

---

## Definition

Curriculum Intelligence analyzes the complete catalog of Programs, Courses, and Units to ensure comprehensive skill coverage and smooth progression.

---

## Capabilities

- **Gap Detection**: Identifies missing competencies or prerequisite skills across program tracks.
- **Redundancy Identification**: Flags overlapping content across courses to encourage asset reuse.
- **Standardization**: Ensures uniform difficulty scaling across departments.

---

# 13. Quality Assurance Intelligence

## Purpose

Monitors content performance and student outcomes to maintain high organizational quality standards.

---

## Definition

Quality Assurance Intelligence automatically monitors live courses for outdated resources, declining engagement, or low assessment pass rates.

---

## Capabilities

- Flags units with unusually low completion rates for tutor review.
- Identifies assessment questions with ambiguous phrasing or abnormal error rates.
- Suggests continuous improvement revisions based on aggregated learner feedback.
