# About This Document

This document defines the **Educational Intelligence Domain Model** for the AI Learning Platform.

Educational Intelligence represents the platform's artificial intelligence capability. Rather than acting as a standalone chatbot or isolated feature, Educational Intelligence is an invisible, continuous co-pilot integrated into every stage of the educational lifecycle.

Its purpose is to improve educational quality, eliminate repetitive tasks, personalize learning, and assist tutors, students, and organizations while keeping educators firmly in control of all decisions.

# SECTION I — PHILOSOPHY & PRINCIPLES

---

# 1. Purpose & Vision

## Purpose

Educational Intelligence exists to elevate educational quality, enable scale, and reduce administrative burden.

It acts as an intelligent assistant for educators and a personalized mentor for students.

---

## Definition

Educational Intelligence is the integrated system of AI capabilities, context-aware algorithms, and data-driven recommendations that operate throughout the platform.

It is human-centered, context-sensitive, and domain-informed.

---

## Key Principles

- Augments human educators; never replaces them.
- Contextual: Learns from organizational standards, course goals, and student profiles.
- Seamless: Embedded in workflows rather than residing in isolated chat windows.

---

# 2. AI Philosophy

## Purpose

Establishes the core belief system guiding how artificial intelligence interacts with educators and learners.

---

## Definition

The AI Philosophy asserts that education is fundamentally a human relationship. Artificial intelligence serves to enhance connection, pedagogical clarity, and learning outcomes.

---

## Core Tenets

- Education comes before technology.
- AI is a teaching assistant and co-pilot.
- Transparency: AI recommendations should always include clear rationales.
- Continuous learning: AI models improve using aggregated engagement feedback.

---

# 3. Human-Centered AI

## Purpose

Ensures educators maintain full ownership, decision-making, and creative control over all educational assets.

---

## Definition

Human-Centered AI means that AI suggestions are proposals, not automated actions.

---

## Business Rules

- AI never automatically publishes educational content without educator approval.
- Tutors can accept, modify, or reject any AI-generated suggestion.
- Authorship and professional identity remain strictly attributed to human educators.

---

# 4. Lifecycle Intelligence

## Purpose

Provides intelligent assistance across every stage of the educational lifecycle.

---

## Definition

Lifecycle Intelligence means AI assistance adapts to where an educator or learner is in their journey—from initial course design to teaching, assessment, and analytics.

---

## Lifecycle Stages

```text
Plan → Create → Review → Publish → Teach → Assess → Analyze → Improve

  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

# SECTION II — PROVIDER INTELLIGENCE

---

# 5. Planning Intelligence

## Purpose

Assists tutors and organizations in curriculum structure, mapping learning outcomes, and identifying prerequisite knowledge.

---

## Capabilities

- **Curriculum Structuring**: Recommends optimal breakdown of Programs into Courses, Units, and Lessons.
- **Outcome Mapping**: Ensures Units align with defined Competencies and target skill levels.
- **Prerequisite Analysis**: Identifies missing foundational topics before course publishing.

---

# 6. Creation Intelligence

## Purpose

Reduces creator friction by drafting explanations, generating activity ideas, and suggesting assessment items.

---

## Capabilities

- **Drafting Assistance**: Suggests explanations, summaries, and reading passages.
- **Activity Generation**: Recommends interactive exercises (quizzes, reflection prompts, coding problems) tailored to lesson objectives.
- **Resource Recommendations**: Suggests relevant worksheets, templates, or visual aids.

---

# 7. Review Intelligence

## Purpose

Evaluates educational content before publication to ensure clarity, completeness, alignment, and quality.

---

## Capabilities

- **Pedagogical Audit**: Evaluates whether lesson objectives match activities and assessments.
- **Clarity & Pacing Check**: Detects overly dense paragraphs, ambiguous questions, or pacing issues.
- **Tone & Standards Check**: Verifies alignment with organization branding and terminology standards.
