# SECTION IV — Shared Provider Capabilities

Educational Providers share a common set of capabilities regardless of whether they operate as an independent tutor or as an organization.

These capabilities provide a consistent foundation across the platform while allowing different provider types to implement them according to their educational and business needs.

Each capability is described in detail within its own dedicated domain document.

---

# 11. Educational Assets

## Purpose

Educational Assets represent the educational value created by an Educational Provider.

They are the primary resources through which providers design, deliver, assess, and continuously improve learning experiences.

Educational Assets are reusable, versioned, and evolve over time.

---

## Examples

Educational Assets include:

- Programs
- Courses
- Units
- Lessons
- Activities
- Assessments
- Resources
- Templates

---

## Shared Principles

Every Educational Provider may:

- create educational assets
- organize educational assets
- publish educational assets
- reuse educational assets
- improve educational assets
- archive educational assets

The educational structure and lifecycle of these assets are defined in the **Core Educational Domain**.

---

# 12. Educational Portfolio

## Purpose

Every Educational Provider maintains an Educational Portfolio that represents their educational achievements, expertise, and contributions.

The portfolio helps learners, organizations, and potential collaborators understand the provider's educational experience and professional reputation.

---

## Typical Portfolio Content

Examples include:

- Biography
- Educational philosophy
- Subjects
- Published Programs
- Published Courses
- Published Units
- Teaching experience
- Certifications
- Reviews
- Achievements
- Educational contributions

---

## Shared Principles

Educational Portfolios should:

- represent professional identity
- evolve over time
- reflect educational quality
- support discoverability
- encourage continuous improvement

Detailed portfolio behaviour is defined within the **Educational Portfolio Model**.

---

# 13. Marketplace Presence

## Purpose

Educational Providers may participate in the Marketplace, allowing learners to discover educational opportunities based on their interests and learning goals.

Marketplace participation is optional.

---

## Marketplace Presence

An Educational Provider may choose to publish:

- Public Profile
- Programs
- Courses
- Units
- Learning Services
- Events
- Workshops
- Learning Communities

---

## Shared Principles

Marketplace participation should:

- promote educational quality
- encourage transparency
- support informed learner decisions
- preserve provider identity
- reward educational excellence

Marketplace behaviour is defined in the **Marketplace & Discovery Model**.

---

# 14. Analytics

## Purpose

Analytics help Educational Providers understand educational performance, learner engagement, and opportunities for improvement.

Analytics support evidence-based educational decision making.

---

## Analytics May Include

Examples include:

- learner engagement
- completion rates
- educational quality
- assessment outcomes
- learner satisfaction
- educational growth
- provider activity
- reputation trends

---

## Shared Principles

Analytics should:

- support continuous improvement
- provide actionable insights
- encourage educational excellence
- remain understandable
- respect privacy

Detailed analytics are defined within the **Analytics Domain**.

---

# 15. Educational Intelligence

## Purpose

Educational Intelligence supports Educational Providers throughout the educational lifecycle.

Rather than replacing educators, Educational Intelligence acts as a collaborative partner that assists planning, creation, review, delivery, assessment, analytics, and continuous improvement.

---

## Educational Intelligence May Assist With

Examples include:

- educational planning
- curriculum review
- content improvement
- assessment review
- learner support
- engagement analysis
- educational recommendations
- continuous improvement

---

## Shared Principles

Educational Intelligence should:

- augment educator expertise
- preserve educator ownership
- promote educational quality
- support organizational knowledge
- encourage continuous improvement

The Educational Intelligence strategy is defined in the **Educational Intelligence Model**.

---

# Shared Capability Relationships

```text
Educational Provider

├── Educational Assets
│
├── Educational Portfolio
│
├── Marketplace Presence
│
├── Analytics
│
└── Educational Intelligence
```

All Educational Providers share these capabilities.

The implementation of each capability may vary depending on whether the provider is a Standalone Tutor or an Organization.
